Market basics in plain words

Nifty 50 and Sensex
Two scoreboards. When most big companies rise, they rise.
Gainers and losers
Stocks that rose or fell the most compared with yesterday's closing price.
Day high and day low
The highest and lowest price a stock touched today. A stock near its high shows buyers are in control today. Near its low, sellers are.
Volume and "volume buzz"
Volume is how many shares changed hands. Volume buzz compares it with the usual amount at the same time of day. 2x means twice the normal activity.
Gap up / gap down
When a stock opens well above or below yesterday's close, often after news. Gaps can shrink or grow during the day.
Intraday range
How far a stock moved from low to high today. Bigger range means bigger risk and bigger possible swings both ways.
Intraday trading
Buying and selling the same day. It is fast and risky. Stop-losses (a pre-set exit price) limit damage but do not remove risk.
NAV
Net Asset Value: the price of one unit of a mutual fund.
CAGR
Average yearly growth rate over several years. 15% CAGR for 3 years means the money grew about 15% each year.
SIP
Systematic Investment Plan: investing a fixed amount every month.
VIX
The fear gauge. A high India VIX means traders expect big swings.
